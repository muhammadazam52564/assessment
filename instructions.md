# Assessment Instructions

Dear Candidate,

I hope you're doing well.

As the next step in our recruitment process, please complete the assessment shared with you. Record your screen for the entire session, because our team will review the recording as part of the evaluation.

We also want to understand how you use AI: how comfortable you are with AI tools and how well you use them to solve problems. Make sure the recording is clear and covers the whole assessment from start to finish.

The assessment details are in `requirements.md`.

## How We Want You to Work: Record Your AI Process

We expect you to use AI tools (Claude or similar) for this assessment, and we want to see how you use them. How you think, give instructions and handle the AI matters as much as the final result.

### What to do

- Record your screen from your very first prompt to the end.
- Show the work as it happens. The recording should include:
  - the instructions you give the AI,
  - how you review its output,
  - where you correct it or push back,
  - how you get around problems.
- If you work across several sessions, record each one and send them in order.

### What not to do

- Don't build everything first and record a walkthrough afterwards.
- Don't send only your prompts or chat history. We want to watch the process live, not read a summary.

### Good to know

- Recordings don't need to be edited or polished. Raw is fine.
- Voice narration is optional, but welcome.

## Deadline

Submit the assessment **within 3 days** of receiving this email. Because of the high volume of applications, your application is dropped automatically after the deadline.

Best of luck!
